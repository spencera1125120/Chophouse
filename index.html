Origin · Vision · Culture

The Story

How we got here. Where we're going.

The Origin
Boone, NC · 2023

Spencer Armstrong founded ChopHouse as an underground house show concept in Boone, NC — averaging 500+ attendees per event and 20,000+ annual gross attendance within its first year. Every gathering reflected a collaborative spirit: immersive lighting setups, themed seasonal shows, a platform for emerging artists, and an inclusive space for community.

The World
What is EDEN?

EDEN is the larger world — a regenerative creative village where culture, sustainability, media, technology, entrepreneurship, and community-building converge into one living system. ChopHouse is the flagship node. This app is its interface: a command center, a game, a scoreboard, and a map of everything being built.

"A living creative ecosystem — part venue, part village, part culture engine."
The Thesis
Underground culture is infrastructure.

Every venue is a node. Every artist is signal. Every attendee is the network. ChopHouse is building the infrastructure for a decentralized culture engine — starting in Boone, then growing wherever the conditions are right. Carbon-neutral approach to event planning. Community as the product. Growth as the mission.

The Roadmap
Era I
The Founding
The comeback show. One loop, one night. Scout the venue, lock the lineup, run the show, capture and reinvest.
Era II
The Engine
The machine. Repeatable shows, n8n automation, content systems, a self-running loop that compounds with every night.
Era III
The Expansion
The network. ChopHouse beyond Boone. Multi-city, real ventures live, a playbook other communities can copy.
